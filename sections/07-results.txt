Stated honestly: on raw cycle counts alone — ignoring whether the design fits — the analytical baseline still wins **10 of 24** kernel–device pairs, mostly the small kernels where everything fits and feasibility never binds. Nine of those ten are raw-cycle losses on spaces PROSE prunes to a coarse front. The tenth is `3mm`, the largest space, where a budget fixed independently of space size is spent before pruning concentrates.

> The claim is not that we produce the fastest circuit on every input. It is that we produce a competitive circuit that is **always realizable**, and we get there far sooner.
