## Regions, not points

So we changed the unit of search. Instead of tracking individual configurations, PROSE tracks **axis-aligned regions**: boxes in knob space, each carrying a constant-size summary — how many configurations it holds, what fraction were feasible, the mean and variance of observed latency, and how many Pareto-optimal designs have been found inside it.

*Constant-size* is the important word. A few dozen boxes stand in for hundreds of millions of points, and memory stays proportional to the number of regions rather than to the evaluation budget.

Each region also carries a confidence interval on its quality, and that interval is what makes the decisions safe.

Nothing is discarded on a hunch. A region is pruned only when its optimistic bound is dominated by another region's conservative bound — and any region already known to hold a Pareto point is shielded outright.
